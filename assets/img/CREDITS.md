# Image credits

The bridal photographs in `assets/img/` are Unsplash originals, supplied as
`slot-*` files and processed into the responsive variants here.

The Unsplash License permits commercial use without attribution, so the demo
is in the clear. **A paying client's live site is a different matter**: these
are other people's photographs of identifiable people, and presenting them as
a salon's own work would be misleading to that salon's customers. Every real
build should ship with the client's own photographs.

`g-jewellery` and `g-palette` are crops of `slot-02`, framed to hold the
gallery to a single session.

`blueprint` is the /blueprint card, cropped hard to the drawing and its
dimension rules. Everything around it — the spec column, the composition
notes, the crop previews — is prose about the *file* ("1200 x 1200 px",
"JPG (sRGB)"), and a salon owner reading that is being shown a production
sheet. It runs at 17% behind the exploded stack, where it is draughting
texture rather than something to read.

`st-*` are the four stage details, each cropped from the region of the shoot
that stage actually produced, so every preview matches the others in light.

There is no skin-prep photograph in this shoot. Four crops were tried and all
were out of focus — in that frame the mehendi hands are the focal plane, not
the face. The stage was cut rather than filled with a blurred face, which also
made the section truer: skin prep happens two weeks before, and the heading
says "what the morning is made of".
