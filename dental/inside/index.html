Smile Dental Care — inside the room

The tray looks worse than the appointment.

Everything laid out for a procedure, and which of it a check-up actually touches. Take any instrument.

The tray

Eight instruments. Three of them see you.

A full tray is set out because it has to be sterile before anyone knows what is needed. Most of it goes back untouched.

Used at a check-up

Mouth mirror

The first thing that goes in, and for a check-up often the only thing. It reflects light onto the back teeth and lets us see behind them.

Yes — every visit

Used at a check-up

Dental probe

Runs along each tooth feeling for soft spots, and measures the gap between tooth and gum. It touches enamel, not nerve.

Yes — every visit

Used at a check-up

Gauze

Keeps a tooth dry while a filling sets. Nothing sticks to a wet tooth.

Yes — every visit

Rarely, and never unannounced

Scissors

For sutures and gauze after surgery. Not part of a check-up.

No — not at a check-up

Extraction forceps

Three shapes for three parts of the mouth. Only ever used on a tooth that is coming out, and only under anaesthetic.

No — not at a check-up

Rarely, and never unannounced

Elevator

Loosens a tooth before it is removed. Sits in the same category as the forceps.

No — not at a check-up

Used at a check-up

Suction tip

Keeps the working area clear. It is the noise most people remember and it does nothing but move air and water.

Yes — every visit

Rarely, and never unannounced

Scalpel handle

Holds a disposable blade for gum surgery. Reserved for procedures we would discuss with you first.

No — not at a check-up

At a routine check-up we use a mirror, a probe and suction. The rest is there because sterilising after the fact is not an option — not because we plan to use it.
