The part nobody mentions

There is no yearly bill.

The reason most clinics never get a site is not the build. It is the fear of a renewal notice every year for something they cannot edit. A .com.np domain is free for registered Nepali businesses, and it renews free, indefinitely. We host on infrastructure that costs nothing to serve a site this size. So there is nothing to cancel and nothing to forget to pay.

Asked so far

The four that come up every time.

I don't want my prices on the internet.

Then they won't be. The demo leads with prices because it is the question patients ask most and almost nobody answers — it is one opinion, argued properly, to show you what having an opinion looks like. It is not the template. Your site can lead with your name and your years instead, or with emergency hours, or with the one treatment you want more of. We will suggest the angle. You decide it.

Who owns it? Am I tied to you?

You do, and no. The domain is registered in your name. The site is a handful of files and you get all of them. If you want to move it somewhere else, or hand it to your nephew who does computers, you take it and go — there is nothing to unlock and no account of ours in the middle.

What happens when my prices change?

Message us and we change them. Small edits — a price, an hour, a new treatment, a photograph — are part of the deal for the first year and we do not bill for them. We would rather your site be right than be finished.

Will this actually bring patients?

Honestly: on its own, slowly. What it does immediately is give the Google Maps listing you already have somewhere to point, so that the people already searching your name find your hours and your treatments instead of nothing. We set up the part that connects the two. The rest is your reviews, which we cannot write and would not.
