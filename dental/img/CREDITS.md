# Dental image credits

Photographs are Pexels originals, supplied alongside three ChatGPT analysis
cards. The Pexels License permits commercial use without attribution, and the
people in these frames are model-released — which settles the consent question
I raised when speccing them. **A paying clinic's live site still needs its own
photographs**: these are other people's rooms and staff, and presenting them as
a clinic's own would mislead that clinic's patients.
