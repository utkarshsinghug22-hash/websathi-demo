# Dental image credits

Photographs are Pexels originals. The Pexels License permits commercial use
without attribution, and the people in these frames are model-released — which
settles the consent question raised when they were specced. **A paying clinic's
live site still needs its own photographs**: these are other people's rooms and
staff, and presenting them as a clinic's own would mislead that clinic's
patients.

| slot | source | why |
|---|---|---|
| `dc-hero`  | pexels-pavel-danilyuk-6812558     | the room with someone working in it — the page opens on a real clinic, not a stock smile |
| `dc-wide`  | pexels-nadezhda-moryak-7800679    | the patient being shown her own X-ray — the page's whole claim, in one frame |
| `dc-room`  | pexels-pavel-danilyuk-6812558     | the entire room. Rooms sell dentistry; close-ups of teeth make people flinch |

All eight are graded to one family: desaturated, cooled slightly, and landed on
a common luminance so they read as one clinic rather than eight stock photos.

## Corrections to the previous version of this file

Two photographs were recorded here as unusable and both notes were wrong.

- `pexels-semihdemirbas88-16098318` was rejected for putting "a patient's face
  near the focal plane". The patient's face is turned away from camera and
  entirely hidden; the dentist is the subject and he is masked. It is now
  `dc-dds`, and it is the most useful frame in the set.
- `pexels-kaplanart-13207320` was rejected on the same grounds. The tray is the
  focal plane and the treatment behind it is heavily defocused. It is now
  `dc-burs`.

`dc-tools` was also attributed to `pexels-cedric-fauntleroy-4269355`; the file
is plainly the kris-tina tray. `cedric-fauntleroy` is the one source in the
folder that is genuinely unused.

## Not photographs

The three `dental_gpt/` files are /anatomy, /blueprint and /explode analysis
cards with callouts drawn onto them, so none can be used as an image. They do
document two of the frames above accurately, and the instrument names on the
/anatomy card were used to check the deep-dive page's tray copy.
